HOW TO PLAY

Move through the arena, use cover, eliminate AI targets and collect supplies. Build coins from eliminations and pickups, then press B to open the armory.

Desktop: WASD/Arrow keys, mouse to aim, left click to fire, right click to aim, R to reload, B for the shop, Esc to release the cursor.
